# Tunes by content (`--tune`)

2026-10-30. x264-style presets that set several options at once by content; options given on the
command line override them. The GUI's 内容 box (なし / アニメ / 実写 / 静止画多め) passes them.

## What each sets

All take 16-frame mini-GOPs where `--bframes` is not given (with `--refs` 5 or more and `--qindex`
100 or more, as the 8-frame default).

| `--tune` | GOP | other |
|---|---|---|
| `animation` | 16 frames where the still share to the last reaches 0.85 (`--gop-still 0.85`; 0.8 until 2026-10-30, see below), else 8 | `--kq -8 --key-boost 64 --level-step-16 2 --deblock-delta 0` |
| `film` | `--gop-still 0.7` | `--deltaq 1` |
| `still` | 16 frames throughout | |

`--gop-still T`: a mini-GOP takes over 8 frames only where the still share (8x8 cells within 2
levels) from its first frame to its last reaches T. `--key-boost` / `--anchor-boost` /
`--level-step` expose the still-share boosts and the pyramid's level step (48 / 24 / 8).

## 600 frames, and stretches mixing quiet and fast scenes (2026-10-30, evening)

`--tune animation` against no tune, 600 frames q100-190 (kaguya 212): on the fhdA.sh stretches
(0-3% of 16-frame windows at still share 0.8 or more, kaguya aside) AM -1.25%, kaguya -16.63%,
Dragon Maid -1.09%, AIR -1.19%, chika -1.73%, Angel Beats -0.56%. On stretches chosen to mix quiet
and fast scenes (about 30% of windows at 0.8 or more; Dragon Maid from 64.7 s, AIR 54.5 s, Angel
Beats 155.4 s, AM 0 s): Dragon Maid 0%, AIR -1.45%, Angel Beats +0.99%, AM +7.19%.

Each part of the tune reverted on the mixed AM / Angel Beats stretches: `--bframes 7` (no 16-frame
mini-GOPs) -4.44 / -1.86%, `--gop-still 0.9` -4.13 / -0.61%, `--key-boost 48` +5.72 / -0.12%,
`--deblock-delta 2` +0.45 / +1.09%. The loss is the 16-frame GOP choice (`--gop-still` looks only
at the first and last frames); 0.9 is no fix, as kaguya lost 10.8% at 0.9 on 120 frames.
Scene-adaptive keyframe offsets: tried-not-kept.md.

Against the tune with 0.8, on the mixed AM / Angel Beats / AIR stretches and kaguya: `--gop-still
0.85` -0.92 / -0.28 / -0.18 / 0.00%, `--level-step 6` -0.54 / +0.05 / -0.44 / +0.15%,
`--anchor-boost 32` +1.59 / -3.17 / -0.35 / +3.91%; 0.85 on the six 600-frame stretches 0.00% but
chika -0.09%. The tune takes 0.85.

Why AM still loses: in its still 16-frame windows the mini-GOP spends 50 KB per 16 frames against
75 KB with two 8-frame ones (q130), but the frames other than the anchor (52 dB) drop from 48.5 to
47.5 dB; at this clip's slope 1 dB is worth about 30% of the bits. The windows are still to their
middle frames (240x135 check), so it is the pyramid's depth. The level step of 16-frame mini-GOPs
only (`--level-step-16`), against the 0.85 tune, mixed AM / Angel Beats / AIR and kaguya: 2
-0.88 / -0.33 / -1.38 / +0.09%, 4 -0.59 / -0.19 / -0.92 / +0.26%, 6 -0.30 / -0.11 / -0.50 /
+0.06%; 2 on the six 600-frame stretches 0.00 to -0.16% (kaguya +0.09%). The tune takes 2. AM's
mixed stretch is still +6% against no tune.
