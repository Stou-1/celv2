## Why it loses (likely)

The term is `| sum |S| - sum |recon| |` over the AC in quantizer steps, added to a squared error in
steps². Over a block with many coefficients the energy difference grows with the coefficient count
while the error per coefficient is under a step, so even S = 0.1 outweighs the error at the
coarser qindexes and buys energy (kept or added AC) with bytes. The loss is largest on live-action
texture (crowd_run, park_joy), where the term is weighted most (texture rule, weight up to 1).

## What would make it worth another try

- A term on the same scale as the error: squared energy difference, or the difference normalised by
  the block's coefficient count, then a fresh strength sweep.
- Stage B (`--psy-trellis`) is measured on S = 0, as the spec's "on the best A".
