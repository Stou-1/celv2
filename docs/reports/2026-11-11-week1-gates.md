# Week 1 gates: G2.2, LR part 3, G1.1 (2026-11-11)

Week 1, item 3 of the plan of 2026-11-11 (`reports/2026-11-11-beat-av1-plan.md` in the shared notes, chapter 2 items 5, 6,
8 and chapter 6). Measurements only: each gate decides whether a feature is built in week 3. Pass or fail is read on
PSNR-YUV 6:1:1 ((6 Y + U + V) / 8, each plane's global PSNR), PSNR-Y beside it.

F0's streams (avmenc without its temporal filter), 33 frames, q100-190, Dragon Maid (md), Angel Beats (ab), AM (am30) and
crowd_run (cr): the only four sources with F0 streams. HEAD ec816ba's oracle build (`--real-trellis` on, the default
now). BD-rate of the second level against the first; + is the second's bytes over the first's. Every one of the 128
streams decodes equal to `--recon`, 33 of 33 frames in F0's list.

## Gate 1, G2.2: per-block interpolation filter

L2r: L2b (avmenc's partitions, modes and MVs, one tile) with the frame's interpolation filter cut to CelV2's
EIGHTTAP_REGULAR (`X_ORACLE_FILT=i`), so no SWITCHABLE frame and no per-block filter. + is what avmenc's filters are worth.

| L2r against L2b | md | ab | am30 | cr | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PSNR-YUV 6:1:1 | +0.96 | +0.02 | +1.20 | +1.30 | +0.87 |
| PSNR-Y | +0.97 | +0.04 | +1.02 | +1.35 | +0.84 |

**G2.2 fails:** AM +1.20% and crowd_run +1.30% (YUV), both under the +1.5% bar; Y reads the same (+1.02, +1.35). This is
avmenc's own choice of filter per block and per frame, so the ceiling: a coder-side choice in CelV2 gets part of it.
Angel Beats is 0, as the candidates report guessed. Not built.

## Gate 2, C2 part 3: luma loop restoration per unit and per class

At L4 (avmenc's decisions and F0's deblocking, CDEF and CCSO, whole 128 blocks, 8 bits), the loop restoration is CelV2's
own fit, not F0's taps (the C2 split's k and w kept F0's taps and overstated):

- **r**: CelV2's restore() on every plane, as CelV2 codes it today: luma PC Wiener in 128 units, each kept by its error;
  chroma one WIENER_NONSEP frame filter per plane.
- **rfk**: chroma as r; luma takes F0's restoration type, unit size and unit types (WIENER_NONSEP, PC Wiener, none,
  SWITCHABLE), with one frame filter fit by CelV2's least squares over F0's WIENER_NONSEP units (subset and dictionary
  match by error less bits).
- **rf**: as rfk with F0's class count (1 to 16), one least-squares filter per class on the decoder's class map.

| | md | ab | am30 | cr | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **rf against r: part 3, YUV** | -0.81 | **-9.49** | **-1.64** | +0.32 | **-2.90** |
| rf against r, Y | -1.06 | -10.91 | -2.06 | +0.38 | -3.41 |
| rfk against r: WIENER_NONSEP on F0's units, YUV | -0.75 | -9.28 | +0.34 | +1.46 | -2.06 |
| rfk against r, Y | -0.97 | -10.73 | +0.38 | +1.66 | -2.41 |
| rf against rfk: classes, YUV | -0.06 | -0.22 | -1.98 | -1.12 | -0.84 |
| rf against rfk, Y | -0.09 | -0.19 | -2.43 | -1.26 | -0.99 |
| Reference: r against L4 (F0's restoration), YUV | +0.48 | +17.71 | +1.53 | -0.24 | +4.87 |
| r against L4, Y | +0.98 | +14.10 | +1.95 | -1.12 | +3.98 |
| Reference: rf against L4, YUV | -0.34 | +6.86 | -0.13 | +0.08 | +1.62 |
| rf against L4, Y | -0.10 | +1.62 | -0.15 | -0.74 | +0.16 |

**LR part 3 passes:** Angel Beats -9.49% and AM -1.64% (YUV), both past the -1% bar; the four sources' mean is -2.90%,
with crowd_run the only one above 0 (+0.32). A -0.3% mean over the 8 sources is plausible: it needs the four sources
without F0 streams (AIR, chika, 吉村, park_joy) to average no worse than +2.3%, while the worst here is +0.32.

- **Two parts, on different sources.** WIENER_NONSEP where CelV2 uses PC Wiener is Angel Beats' (-9.28) and Dragon
  Maid's (-0.75); classes are AM's (-1.98) and crowd_run's (-1.12). crowd_run loses with one class (+1.46) and gains it
  back with F0's classes.
- **Angel Beats' gain is not one frame's.** At q130 the luma error falls over all the frames (the largest share of the drop,
  display frame 4, is 11%; q190: 10%), and 85% of the bytes saved (q190: 76%) are in the first anchor (display 17), whose
  quality the pyramid then carries. The candidates report's caution (81% of the L4e saving in one frame) does not hold here.
- **The refit matches F0's own taps on luma.** rf against L4 is Y -0.10 to -0.74% on three sources, +1.62 on Angel Beats:
  the fit is not what is left. The YUV gap on Angel Beats (+6.86 against +1.62 on Y) is chroma, where CelV2's one frame
  filter per plane loses to F0's per-unit chroma taps (U at q130: 41.05 against 42.72 dB). That is outside part 3; noted
  for a later look.
- **Read with care.** These are F0's unit types, unit sizes and class counts, as the C2 split's rows are F0's choices:
  CelV2's own search for them will get part of this. F0 codes per-unit taps (no frame filter) in 3-16 of Angel Beats'
  33 frames (by q) and 0-10 of the other sources'; here those frames take frame filters, so per-unit taps are not measured.

Built in week 3 by the plan, with the offset of week 2 for its CPU.

## Gate 3, G1.1: big intra in inter frames

L0 (CelV2's own decisions on F0's frame list, the encoder's defaults otherwise, 10 bits, two tile columns) with and
without `X_G11`: the RD pass of inter frames also scores 64x64, 64x32 and 32x64 intra in all 13 modes, as intra frames'
does (`--intra64`, the same x1.1 bias against them). Large-block inter candidates are as they are.

| L0 + G1.1 against L0 | md | ab | am30 | cr | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PSNR-YUV 6:1:1 | -0.62 | **-0.15** | +0.22 | -0.03 | -0.15 |
| PSNR-Y | -0.63 | +0.05 | +0.15 | -0.04 | -0.12 |

**G1.1 fails:** Angel Beats gains 0.15% (YUV; Y +0.05), under the 0.5% the gate asks, so it stops here. Dragon Maid's
-0.62% is the one gain, short of the build bar's -1% too. The ceiling L2 against L1 (-15% on Angel Beats) is not in the
intra blocks the RD pass can now pick: avmenc's modes come with its partitions and MVs. Not built.

## Reproduce

Oracle build (`-DCELV2_ORACLE=ON`, as celv2-bench/l1l2's `build_oracle.ps1`) at ec816ba, into
`celv2-bench/l1l2/w1gates/build_oracle`. In celv2-bench/l1l2/w1gates (scripts in `py/`, l12.py's runs with Y, U and V
PSNR; the rows of this report in `g.csv`):

`LEVELS=l2b,l2r,l4we,l4rwe,l4rfwe,l4rfkwe,l0,l0g python -I py/gates.py 8`, then `python -I py/table.py` (tables.py's
`bd()` on YUV 6:1:1 and Y). `py/perframe.py ab 130 l4rwe l4rfwe` gives the per-frame reading; `py/lrstat.py` F0's luma
restoration per frame from an oracle_dump file. The oracle dumps are l1l2's `d6/`.
