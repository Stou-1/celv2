# `--warp` stage A (2026-11-05): no gain yet; stage B's conditions hold

Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-warp-delta-design.md. Built in 1ced58a (`--warp 0|1`, default 0).

- avmenc gains on every source, regular ones too, so warp-delta is not a Flash-only tool.
- 70-80% of that gain comes from WARP_NEWMV with real rotation and zoom models, the syntax CelV2 already writes.
- CelV2 gains nothing: it codes warp on 0.01-0.07% of the area, where avmenc codes 30-40%.
- Both of stage B's conditions hold. Stage B is its own spec, so this stops here and goes to the design session.
- `--warp` stays at 0. Flash's -10% goal is not met.

All BD-rates are by global-MSE PSNR-Y at 10 bits, q100-190, 33 frames (Flash 30), negative is better.

## What avmenc's warp is

Counted from the streams at q160 (tools/wacct.c, an AVM inspection build). "Area" is the share of the frame's luma.

| | Flash | 吉村 | AIR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Area coded with warp-delta | 30.5% | 39.8% | 7.4% |
| Warp area that is WARPMV | 93% | 92% | 58.5% |
| WARPMV area with an identity model | 60% | 76% | 7.5% |

The identity blocks look like the larger part, but they are worth almost nothing. A throwaway avmenc split the gain
(its plain setting gave Flash q190 byte for byte the normal encoder's stream):

| Allowed | Flash | 吉村 | AIR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everything | -9.62% | -3.08% | -4.28% |
| No WARPMV with an identity model | -9.38% | -2.91% | -4.03% |
| No WARPMV at all | -7.11% | -2.30% | -3.42% |
| Identity models only | -0.33% | -0.45% | -0.25% |

- **WARP_NEWMV alone gives 74-80% of the gain.** Real models carry it.
- **WARPMV adds 0.8-2.5%.** The spec's "Not in this" says within 0.7% on Flash. That figure compared switches, and
  WARPMV is on whenever warp-delta is on, so the spec's -19.6% includes WARPMV.
- **Without WARPMV, Flash's warp is 13% of the inter area.** 72% of it (吉村 58%) takes a neighbour's model at
  `warp_ref_idx` 1, which codes no delta; only 16% (吉村 34%) codes its own at index 0. The models are large: on Flash
  82% are over 1/32. A few blocks find a model and the others inherit it.
- **Block sizes:** avmenc's WARP_NEWMV-only warp sits in 32 and 64 blocks (3.5% and 5.7% of the area).

**A flaw in 1ced58a, left in:** `choose_warp()` bounds each trial by the block's best, and pred_rd() returns a partial sum
once over the bound. When the first model loses to translation, later partial sums pass as improvements and the one-step
moves wander. Fixing it changes nothing measurable (first row of the coder changes above), so it is not the cause.

## Stage B's conditions

- **Flash gains less than half of avmenc's:** yes, +0.01% against -9.62%.
- **The share where each term +-4 steps beats the fit by 10% or more reaches 20%:** yes, 48% at q100 (3,745 of 7,824
  blocks) and 53% at q160 (5,203 of 9,847). Scored by the coder's residual cost, not SATD, since that is what the coder
  compares; before any bits, MV fixed.

From the same count at q160: the fit beats the block's translation in 2,209 blocks and the +-4 search in 3,000, against 11
that win once the bits are counted. So each block's win is small against about 30 bits.

## Why CelV2 gets so little: not settled

Measured:
- **The search reaches 19% of the area** (Flash q160). Skip-mode and compound decisions never try warp, and they hold
  64.5% of the inter area, against avmenc's 45%.
- **The seed problem:** with almost no block coding a model, the warp candidate list is nearly empty, so the cheap
  inherited case avmenc lives on never starts.

Not measured, the likely order to look:
1. Warp in the partition and mode decision, at every block size, as AVM does (rdopt.c, on the real references). The engine
   decides on translation, and the coder only sees what that left.
2. The MV fit: 8x8 engine MVs are noisy (a 0.37, b 0.15 on the zoom clip), and 48-53% of fits are 10% short of a nearby
   model.

The one-reference run does not settle the first point: skip mode still took 84% of the inter area there.

## Not done against the spec

- No frame gate, no RD pass scoring in the code, no 6 parameters: all wait on a gain to gate.
- The gate test, and a speed measurement on a quiet machine.

## Stage A2's counts (2026-11-05, after the design answers)

**Count 1, what inheriting is worth.** A throwaway avmenc with `warp_ref_idx` 0 only:

| Allowed | Flash | 吉村 | AIR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everything | -9.62% | -3.08% | -4.28% |
| Index 0 only | -8.25% | -2.86% | -3.51% |
| No WARPMV | -7.11% | -2.30% | -3.42% |
| No WARPMV, index 0 only | -5.58% | -2.11% | -3.14% |

Inheriting is worth 7-22% of the warp gain, under the spec's quarter, so the design session looks again before step 2 (seeds).
A block's own model alone still gives Flash -5.58%, past the -5% goal.

**Count 2, block sizes.** Over avmenc's WARP_NEWMV area at Flash q160, CelV2's blocks are smaller on 62.6% (70.1% in the
stream without WARPMV), past the half that opens the merge candidate.

**Step 1's ceiling.** Rather than build the reach first: no skip mode and no compound at all, so every inter block reaches
`choose_warp()`, with the search's bound fixed, 4 passes, MV descent and kWarpBias 2; `--warp 1` against `--warp 0` under the same
switch:

| | Flash | 吉村 |
|---|---|---|
| BD-rate | +0.82% | -1.21% |
| Time | 1.07x | 1.36x |
| Warp on the inter area, Flash q160 | 0.9% (avmenc 13% without WARPMV) | |

So reach is not what limits Flash: with every block in reach warp still wins under 1% of the area. 吉村 gains, at a cost.
What is left points at count 2: the engine splits a rotating area on translation, and a 16x16 piece of it gains too little
from a model to pay its bits. Step 1 is not built; this goes to the design session with the counts.
