## The gate

Logs at q130: Flash's palette share 0.043-0.235; regular sources at most 0.031 (Dragon Maid), the rest 0.010.

## Against the spec

- The repeat share does not open the gate: it overlaps (Flash 0.02-0.25; Angel Beats up to 0.40, AM demo 0.17,
  AIR 0.15), and those sources gain nothing with the tools on.
- The gate test's natural frame is `scene_frame` with a capture's noise: bare, its two-level stripes score 0.117,
  and no threshold keeps that off and Flash on.
- IntraBC scores against the decided intra mode, not the luma search's best (use_intrabc comes first in the stream).
- Near repeats (hashes on coarser samples) were measured and dropped: tried-not-kept.

## Why Flash stops short (a reading, not a measurement)

Stage A takes blocks of at most 8 exact values and exact repeats. Flash here is a lossy capture, so few blocks
qualify: a median of 8-17% of 16x16s by the palette share. avmenc's -13.4% comes with colour clustering and a
real block search. The spec's palette rule keeps the k-means for blocks that already have 8 values or fewer, so
going further is a design question: palettes for blocks over 8 values, with a residual; near-match IntraBC
searched per block (B1 / B2 are written for halftone, not for this).
