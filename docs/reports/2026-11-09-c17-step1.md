## What changed (src/core/rd_int.h, .cpp)

- **Units** per superblock qindex: distortion in J = 1/(1024 dqv^2) of a squared step, bits in 1/512 bit, a trial's
  J = distortion + lam x bits, lam = llround(2 w dqv^2). Side bits are exact (x512); palette, IntraBC and
  `block_bits` round once. Sums saturate at 2^62 (`rdi::add`); x1.1 for 64-size intra is J + J / 10.
- **TCQ** as tcq_levels(): X2 = |c| << (4 + txs), candidates floor(X2 / 2dqv) and floor((X2 + dqv) / 2dqv) by an
  exact multiply, terms ((X2 - rec2)^2 << 8) + lam bits(l), the eight states, their pairs and tie rules, --rd-zero's
  fresh starts and all-zero end, missing candidates and unreachable states at 2^61 (clamped after every position).
  An AVX2 int64 form and a scalar reference, equal on 7200 test blocks (every size, six qindexes, ties).
- **The plain path** (`--tcq 0`) and --rd-zero's whole-block choice, transliterated.
- **The sums**: distortion against the decoder's dequantization, `(c - dq)^2 << (16 + 2 txs)`, plus a 64-point
  transform's dropped energy in the same units; bits of the levels before the end and of the end itself.
- `--psy-trellis` takes quantize()'s levels and the same sums; `--psy-rd`'s term rounds once into J.

On the same coefficients the integer and double quantizers pick the same levels on 98-100% of blocks (the rest:
ties under the rounded bits; S1 dump, every size).

## The integer transform: built, measured, not kept

`rdi::fdct()` (AVM's AVX2 DCT2 and its own 64-point upper halves) stays with its tests and `tools/c17` benches as
the start of the GPU port, but the RD pass does not use it:

| Integer transform in the RD pass | BD-rate |
|---|---|
| AVM's shifts and gain | +0.24% mean, AIR +0.91% |
| ... with the first pass's shift moved to the second | AIR +0.64% |
| ... at the coder's scale per frequency (Q14 from AVM's row norms), rounded once, the 64-point dropped energy by row norms | AIR +0.18, AM +0.10, chika +0.14, Angel Beats +0.38% |
| the float transform, integer costs (kept) | -0.03% mean |

- AVM's forward gain is up to 0.6% above the coder's scale (which inverts the decoder's DC gain) on 2:1 sizes, and its
  rows' norms are off by up to 1% a pass: fixed per frequency.
- What stays is the matrices' leakage (up to 0.4%). For a 64-point transform the dropped energy is 65-92% of the
  distortion, so a small error there moves the 64 against 32x32 choice; each way of computing it moved one anime
  source or another by 0.3-1%.
- So the GPU port (on hold) needs an integer DCT nearer orthonormal than AVM's (Q13+ matrices), checked against
  fwd_dct() the same way before it changes any bytes. A row in tried-not-kept.

Two bugs found on the way, both by comparing costs against the old ones (`c17_bench <dump> <frames> cmp`):
- sqrt(2)'s direction in the dropped rows' energy (32x64 at half);
- the coefficients at AVM's own gain.

## Speed

The quantizer and the sums are the gain (no divisions over every position); the transform is today's. CPU a frame
-3 to -7% (above).
