| coder NEWMV: rd_cost_ calls a search, repeats | 21.6, 25.4% | 22.7, 15.2% | 21.8, 22.6% | 22.5, 17.4% |
| 64-size intra pick outside SATD's best 3 / 4 | 44.4 / 34.6% | 1.3 / 1.0% | 47.8 / 41.3% | 26.5 / 23.0% |

(`--preset max` on AM 33: the `--rd-intra` loop's pick is outside SATD's best 3 / 4 in 20.9 / 14.2% of 7.07 M blocks.)

- **C20's memo should be keyed by the residual, not by the block and MVs.** No `inter_cost` trial repeats (the
  `tried` list already removes them), but identical residuals repeat a lot: up to 73% of the transformed samples
  on flat anime (Dragon Maid), 38% on S1. A cost depends only on the residual, the size and the frame's
  parameters (without `--psy-*`), so a content-keyed memo is exact. Many repeats are probably all-zero
  residuals (not counted apart yet); a zero check is cheaper than a hash.
- **C19 (a) as specified fails its own gate.** The RD pass's 64-size intra pick is outside SATD's best 3 in 26-48%
  of blocks on AM, S1 and 吉村; the spec asked for a small miss rate. Not built; a wider shortlist (6-8) is
  the only form left, for half or less of the saving.
- The coder's NEWMV repeats (15-25% of 22 calls a block) are real but the coder's whole transform and
  quantization work is 5-9% of the CPU: C20's coder half is worth under 2%.

## The Vulkan engine's kernels (`CELV2_VK_TIMES=1`)

GPU time per kernel, summed over the encode, from time stamps with a barrier on each side. Timed work runs one
dispatch at a time, so these are each kernel's own time, and the totals are above an untimed run's GPU time.
Without the barriers the RTX 5090 overlaps dispatches and wrote its stamps early (a row's costs read 0.7 us).
The B50 runs one dispatch at a time anyway: its numbers were the same both ways.

ms a frame (AM 33 frames at 1080p; a16k: 9 frames at 16K, `--workers 1`):

| Kernel | 5090, refs 2 | 5090, refs 7 | B50, refs 2 | B50, refs 7 | 5090 16K | B50 16K |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| me (motion search) | 8.4 | 18.4 | 170 | 371 | 262 | 6339 |
| cost inter | 11.1 | 24.0 | 99 | 225 | 264 | 3984 |
| cost intra | 3.8 | 3.8 | 45 | 47 | 145 | 2636 |
| finalize | 13.0 | 13.8 | 26 | 28 | 267 | 1621 |
| subpel, copies, downsample | 0.7 | 0.7 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 32 | 59 |
| **total** | **37** | **61** | **343** | **674** | **970** | **14639** |
| encode fps | 4.89 | 4.38 | 2.38 | 1.31 | 0.151 | 0.054 |

- **The B50's motion search is its outlier.** A call takes 12.4 ms against the 5090's 0.6 ms (20x), while
  `cost inter` is 9.4x, about the two GPUs' compute ratio. The motion search is 55% of the B50's engine time at
  1080p with 7 references, and 43% at 16K. C23's `me.slang` item (the block in registers, the window in
  groupshared memory, no scratch arrays) comes first.
- **The RTX 5090's per-row dispatches are short and underfilled.** `finalize` takes 0.8 ms a row on 30
  workgroups, about half of `cost inter`'s time. GPU time is about 61 ms a frame against the encode's 228 ms a
  frame per worker, so the 5090 is not the limit at 1080p. C23's `kShares` sweep and merging `finalize` into the
  costs' dispatch are for 16K, where the 5090's engine is 970 ms a frame.
- engine_bench (10 frames, refs 7): 5090 70.1 ms a frame wall, 21 ms of it in kernels; B50 758 ms, 234 ms in
  kernels. Most of the 5090's engine time is not kernel time; the B50's other two thirds are not explained
  yet (host waits, the upload of 7 references per frame in engine_bench).

## What this changes in the order

1. **C20 first, keyed by the residual.** It is the largest measured repeat: 38% of S1's transformed samples, 73%
   of Dragon Maid's. The memo goes in `tx_cost()`: an all-zero residual answered without the transform, and other
   residuals by a hash checked against the stored residual, per superblock and thread. `--psy-*` bypass it. Exact:
   same bytes. The coder half (NEWMV) is dropped: under 2%.
2. **Then C18 (c), then (a), (b), (d), (e)** as the spec has them. TCQ is the largest function group (17% of AM's
   samples), the DCT next (14%).
3. **C19 (a) is not built:** the miss rate gate fails (row in tried-not-kept).
4. **C17 passes its gate:** 71-73% of the CPU is the RD pass with its transforms and quantization.
5. **C23 starts with `me.slang` on the B50**, then the 5090's row dispatches at 16K.

**The sampler and the profile disagree on the in-loop filters.**
- `tools/sampler` on S1 (`--workers auto`) puts the filter searches at about 20% of the CPU samples:
  - chroma Wiener statistics (`codec_state.cpp:1290`) 6.5%;
  - CCSO's search (`ccso_search.cpp:103`) 5.3%;
  - CDEF's filters and search about 8%.
- `CELV2_PROFILE` puts them at about 10%.
- The sampler also puts the coder's `tcq_cost` above the RD pass's `tcq_levels`.

The profile is the one that fits the measured memo:
- C20 removed 38% of the RD pass's transform samples and saved 13.8% of the process's CPU time.
- That needs the RD pass's transforms and quantization near the profile's 51%, not the sampler's.

The sampler undercounts threads that live one frame, which is how the RD pass runs its threads. It now looks for
new threads every 5 ticks instead of 50, and drops threads that have ended, but the count stays about the same.
Lambdas now print their source line.
